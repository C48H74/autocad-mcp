# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome regardless of background, identity or experience. Be respectful, discuss technical disagreements constructively and give actionable feedback.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of personal information and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation or block accounts when necessary. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content; do not publish private details. This is the project's own concise conduct policy.
